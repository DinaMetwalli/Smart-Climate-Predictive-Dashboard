About The Dashboard


Dashboard Aim

The aim of the Smart Climate Dashboard is to predict and visualise continental average surface temperature anomalies based on current trends, creating an ML-based dashboard to assist with environmental initiatives through user interaction.

How It Works

The dashboard uses an LSTM (Long-Short Term Memory) Artificial Neural Network trained on NOAA and Our World In Data's continental surface temperature datasets to produce a 60-month future forecast across 6 continents, communicated through interactive visualisations.

What You Can Do

  • Reduce plastic use and waste.
